August Events

We have a variety of events planned for the month of August to help our members stay active and engaged. From yoga workshops to community gatherings, there's something for everyone to enjoy.

Here are some of the events we have lined up for August:

  • Yoga Retreat: Join us for a weekend retreat in a serene location, where you can deepen your practice and connect with like-minded individuals.
  • Workshops: We regularly host workshops on various yoga styles, meditation techniques, and wellness topics to help you expand your knowledge and skills.
  • Community Events: We organize community events such as charity yoga classes, outdoor yoga sessions, and social gatherings to foster a sense of community among our members.

Highlighted Events

In addition to our regular events, we also have some special highlighted events that we are excited to share with you. These events are designed to provide unique experiences and opportunities for growth and connection.

Here are some of our highlighted events that you won't want to miss:

  • Summer Membership Retreat: We host in the summer time, a membership retreat to close parks and sometimes beaches to detox the body, relax the senses to start anew with our members.
  • VIP Hot Springs Access: On Saturdays, members of the VIP get to enjoy our hot springs that are open during winters and sometimes fall seasons.
  • Spring Wellness Program: During spring, our basic members are allowed limited access to our gyms' VIP spa suite to enjoy and treat themselves to relaxation and more!

Be sure to check our events calendar regularly for updates on upcoming events and workshops. We look forward to seeing you there!
