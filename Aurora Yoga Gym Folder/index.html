About Us

Aurora Yoga Gym, was founded in 2011 by a young inspiring woman, Katie Slay, who realized that not many people are inspired to stay healthy and active. Wanting to spread self awareness to others, Katie and her friends that share the same goal created this gym.

Our instructors are passionate about helping our members find balance and improve their overall well-being. We believe that yoga is not just a physical practice, but also a mental and spiritual one.
